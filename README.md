# Nuvaro Agro Pvt Ltd - B2B Dehydrated Powders Web Platform

Modern, responsive B2B website and commercial administrative application for **Nuvaro Agro Pvt Ltd**, a manufacturer and bulk exporter of dehydrated vegetable, fruit, herbal, and spice powders.

---

## 🎨 Design System (Wellness B2B Transformation)

- **Brand Palette:**
  - Deep Forest Green: `#1F4D36`
  - Warm Cream Background: `#F5F1E6`
  - Mustard Gold Buttons & Accents: `#C9972B`
  - Clean white cards with soft shadows (`shadow-card`, `shadow-elevated`)
  - Subtle leaf graphics, rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`)
  - Typography: Poppins & Inter
- **B2B Transformation:** Replaced consumer "Add to Cart" with **"Request Quote"** & **"Request Sample"**, batch-specific COAs, technical specifications, MOQs, and turnkey private labeling.

## 📑 Complete Page & Section Architecture

### 1. Homepage (14 Ordered Sections)
1. **Sticky Navbar:** Brand logo with leaf insignia, navigation links, quick search, compare indicator, "Request a Quote" gold button, and Admin Portal link.
2. **Hero Section:** “Premium Dehydrated Powders for Food, Nutraceutical & Beverage Brands”, CTAs for Commercial Quote & Technical Catalogue, visual powder showcase.
3. **Trust Strip:** ISO 22000:2018 / HACCP / FSSAI Central / US FDA, Lab Tested (COA with every batch), Export Ready (50+ countries), Custom Specifications.
4. **Product Categories Grid (6 Cards):** Vegetable, Fruit, Herbal & Ayurvedic, Spice Powders, Superfood Blends, Custom Formulations.
5. **Best-Selling Powders Grid:** Image, botanical name, drying method, mesh size, MOQ, "Request Quote", "Spec Sheet" preview, and "Add to Compare".
6. **Private Label Banner:** “Your Formula. Our Powder.” with turnkey contract blending CTA.
7. **Why Choose Us (5 Icon Blocks):** Farm-sourced raw material, controlled drying kinetics, 100% batch traceability, bulk food-grade packaging, on-time global maritime & air delivery.
8. **Farm-to-Powder Manufacturing Timeline:** 6-step visual manufacturing process (Harvest -> Ozone Wash -> Gentle Dehydration -> Micronizing -> Magnetic Metal Detection -> Cleanroom Nitrogen Packing).
9. **Industries We Serve:** Food & Beverage, Nutraceuticals, Bakery & Confectionery, Baby Food, Pet Food, Cosmetics, Snacks & Seasonings.
10. **Sample Offer Banner:** “Get a Free 100g Sample for Your Lab Trial” (dispatched within 48h via DHL/FedEx).
11. **Client Testimonials:** Sourcing heads and VP R&D reviews from Germany, USA, UAE, and South Korea.
12. **Latest Blog / Resources:** Drying technology comparisons, COA reading guide, and global supply chain forecasts.
13. **Newsletter / Price List Inquiry:** Corporate email capture for monthly wholesale FOB/CIF price index updates.
14. **Footer:** Quick links, product portfolios, factory address, WhatsApp direct link, certifications, and export gateways.

---

### 2. Product Catalog & Details
- **Multi-Facet Filtered Listing:** Live search, filter by category, drying technology (spray, freeze, air, drum), mesh size (60, 80, 100 mesh), and export certifications.
- **Product Detail Deep-Dive:**
  - High-res photo gallery (fine powder & raw botanical produce)
  - Interactive specification tabs: Physical & Chemical parameters, Microbiological limits (TPC, Yeast & Mould, E. coli, Salmonella), Nutritional breakdown per 100g, Bulk packaging formats (5kg, 10kg, 25kg drums).
  - Downloadable COA, Technical Data Sheet (TDS), and Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS).
  - Embedded Fast Quotation form.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Tool:** Compare specifications of up to 3 powders simultaneously.

---

### 3. B2B Commercial Admin Application
- **Lead Kanban Pipeline:** 5-stage status pipeline (`New`, `Contacted`, `Quoted`, `Won`, `Lost`) with drag/status updates and inquiry notes.
- **Inquiry Master Table:** Full search, status filters, and detailed view modal.
- **Official PDF Quotation Generator:** Interactive quotation builder that generates official CIF/FOB proforma sheets on Nuvaro Agro letterhead with print-to-PDF formatting.
- **Export Analytics:** Top demanded powders, destination country breakdowns, and quality clearance benchmarks.

## 🚀 Running the Project

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start Vite development server
npm run dev

# Build production bundle
npm run build
```

Open `http://localhost:5173` to explore the live website and Admin Portal.
